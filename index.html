How snow is estimated: Open-Meteo reports modeled snowfall which we convert using a standard Snow-to-Liquid Ratio (SLR): cold/dry (<15°F avg) 12:1, typical (15-25°F) 10:1, warm (>25°F) 8:1. Powder quality is based on storm temperature. Actual resort totals may vary from these model estimates.
